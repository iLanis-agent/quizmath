Quiz Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts a quiz night with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the rounds a set of questions makes, the points, the running time, and a score as a percent. It uses no named constants. The questions, the questions a round, the points, the bonus, the seconds a question, the break length and the score are yours to decide and read off your own quiz sheet and answer key. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your quiz or your players and does not know whether the questions are fair or the night will run to time, and it gives no verdict that a score is good or a quiz is balanced. Every number here is your own entry.

The rounds truth

Rounds: the questions divided by the questions a round, rounded up, with the size of the last round.

The points truth

Points: the questions times the points each, plus the bonus for each round.

The score truth

Score: the correct answers over the total, as a percent to one decimal place, half rounded up.

The time truth

Time: the questions times the seconds each, plus a break between each pair of rounds.

Quiz Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
