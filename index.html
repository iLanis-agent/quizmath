Quiz Math

The 38 questions at 8 a round make 5 rounds, the last round holding 6.

Boundary: arithmetic only Quiz Math counts a quiz night with multiplication, division and rounding-up: the rounds a set of questions makes, the points, the running time, and a score as a percent. It uses no named constants. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your quiz or your players and does not know whether the questions are fair or the night will run to time, and it gives no verdict that a score is good or a quiz is balanced. Every number here is your own entry.

Starting examples: 38 questions, 8 a round, 2 points each with a 3 point round bonus, 23 correct, 45 seconds a question and 10 minute breaks.

The points truth

The 38 questions at 2 points each are worth 76 points, and 5 rounds at 3 bonus add 15, 91 in all.

The score truth

23 correct of 38 is 60.5%.

The time truth

The 38 questions at 45 seconds each with 4 breaks of 10 minutes take 4110 seconds, 1 hour 8 minutes 30 seconds.
